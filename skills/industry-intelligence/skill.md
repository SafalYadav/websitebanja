# WebsiteBanja AI — Industry & Vertical Design Intelligence Skill

> **Role**: Domain Context Reasoning, Vertical Information Architecture, Customer Intent Mapping, and Sector-Specific Conversion Engineering.  
> **Source**: Empirical conversion data across 15+ industry verticals, sector consumer psychographics, commercial UX benchmarks, and business operational realities.  
> **Application Priority**: Industry-Specific Operational Reality > Standard Website Conventions > Generic Design Templates.

---

## 1. Core Philosophy: The Law of Vertical Differentiation

A restaurant website is fundamentally NOT a SaaS platform. A plumbing company is NOT an art portfolio. Forcing every business into the same section structure (Hero $\rightarrow$ 3 Cards $\rightarrow$ Pricing $\rightarrow$ FAQ) is the defining flaw of amateur AI builders.

WebsiteBanja AI diagnoses the **true customer intent** for each business vertical and constructs an information hierarchy tailored to how customers in that specific industry make buying decisions.

## 2. Deep Intelligence Across Major Industry Verticals

### 1. Restaurant, Fine Dining & Hospitality
- **Primary Customer Intent**: "Can I see the food? What are the prices? Is there a table available tonight? Where is it located?"
- **Primary Conversion Action**: `"Reserve A Table"` (with date/time/guests) or `"Order Online for Delivery"`.
- **Secondary Action**: `"View Dinner Menu"` or `"Call (555) 019-2834"`.
- **Critical Information Hierarchy**:
  1. Hero: Mouth-watering food photography, atmosphere teaser, direct reservation widget.
  2. The Menu: Categorized (Starters, Mains, Desserts, Wine List) with clear dish names, ingredients, dietary flags (Vegan, GF), and prices.
  3. Atmosphere / Chef Story: Warm provenance narrative, interior dining room photography.
  4. Essential Logistics: Operating hours, exact street address, interactive Google Maps link, parking info.
- **Visual Tone**: Warm, sensory, organic, appetizing. Rich creams, dark woods, or crisp editorial white.
- **Mobile Priority**: Click-to-call phone, one-tap navigation directions, instant table booking.

---

### 2. B2B SaaS, Cloud & Developer Platforms
- **Primary Customer Intent**: "What technical problem does this solve? How does it integrate with my stack? What does it cost? Is it secure?"
- **Primary Conversion Action**: `"Start 14-Day Free Trial"` (Self-serve) or `"Book an Enterprise Demo"` (High-ACV).
- **Secondary Action**: `"View Interactive Product Tour"` or `"Read API Documentation"`.
- **Critical Information Hierarchy**:
  1. Hero: Crystal-clear value proposition, dual CTAs, high-fidelity application UI preview with ambient glow.
  2. Social Proof: Customer logo wall ("Trusted by teams at Stripe, Vercel, Figma").
  3. Feature Showcase (Bento Grid): Asymmetrical technical capability cards, workflow diagrams, code blocks.
  4. Security & Compliance: SOC2 Type II, ISO 27001, GDPR compliance badges.
  5. 3-Tier Pricing Table: Monthly/annual switch, transparent limits, feature comparison matrix.
- **Visual Tone**: Precision-engineered, clean, dark-mode ambient or modern high-contrast white, monospace metadata chips.
- **Mobile Priority**: Rapid comprehension of the core value proposition; frictionless trial signup.

### 3. Local Home Services & Emergency Trades (Plumbing, HVAC, Electrical)
- **Primary Customer Intent**: "My basement is flooding right now. Are you licensed? How fast can you get here? Can I trust you?"
- **Primary Conversion Action**: `"Call (555) 019-2834 — 24/7 Dispatch"` (Click-to-Call).
- **Secondary Action**: `"Request Free Online Estimate"`.
- **Critical Information Hierarchy**:
  1. Hero: Direct statement of emergency capability, immediate phone button, guaranteed arrival window ("On-site within 45 mins").
  2. Trust Badges: State License #, Bonded & Insured, BBB A+ rating, 4.9-Star Google rating with 500+ reviews.
  3. Core Service Areas: Bulleted list of common emergencies (Burst Pipes, Water Heaters, Drain Snaking).
  4. Service Territory: Explicit list of zip codes and cities covered.
  5. Upfront Pricing Reassurance: "No hidden dispatch fees • Flat-rate upfront estimates".
- **Visual Tone**: High-trust, direct, clean, dependable. Bold navy blue, safety yellow or clean red accents.
- **Mobile Priority**: Large sticky bottom call button; phone number clickable across all viewports.

---

### 4. Healthcare, Dental & Wellness Clinics
- **Primary Customer Intent**: "Can this doctor solve my pain? Are they qualified? Do they accept my insurance? Is the clinic clean?"
- **Primary Conversion Action**: `"Book An Appointment Online"`.
- **Secondary Action**: `"Check Accepted Insurance Plans"`.
- **Critical Information Hierarchy**:
  1. Hero: Welcoming clinic imagery, warm compassionate headline, new patient booking CTA.
  2. Provider Credentials: Doctor/practitioner headshots, medical degrees, board certifications, specialties.
  3. Treatment Capabilities: Plain-language explanations of procedures (Preventive, Cosmetic, Restorative).
  4. Patient Reviews: Real testimonials emphasizing painless procedures and caring bedside manner.
  5. Insurance & Payment: Transparent list of in-network insurance providers and flexible financing plans.
- **Visual Tone**: Soothing, hygienic, tranquil, accessible. Soft teal, clean white, calm sage green, accessible contrast.
- **Mobile Priority**: Instant patient portal booking and emergency clinic telephone line.

---

### 5. Legal Practices & Financial Consultancies
- **Primary Customer Intent**: "Does this firm win cases like mine? How much experience do they have? Can I speak to a senior attorney?"
- **Primary Conversion Action**: `"Schedule Confidential Consultation"`.
- **Secondary Action**: `"Review Case Results & Verdicts"`.
- **Critical Information Hierarchy**:
  1. Hero: Authoritative statement of practice, attorney group portrait, confidential case evaluation form.
  2. Measurable Track Record: Stat numbers ("$45M+ Recovered for Clients", "25+ Years Courtroom Experience").
  3. Practice Areas: Distinct cards detailing specialized legal domains (Corporate Law, Personal Injury, Family Estate).
  4. Attorney Profiles: Senior partner backgrounds, bar associations, courtroom honors.
  5. Client Confidentiality Notice: Explicit reassurance that inquiries are privileged and confidential.
- **Visual Tone**: Authoritative, prestigious, serious, conservative. Deep navy, forest green, warm charcoal, serif headlines.
- **Mobile Priority**: Quick confidential case submission form and direct office telephone.

---

### 6. Creative Agencies, Indie Designers & Portfolios
- **Primary Customer Intent**: "How good is their aesthetic taste? What notable clients have they worked with? Are they available?"
- **Primary Conversion Action**: `"Start a Project"` or `"Get In Touch"`.
- **Secondary Action**: `"Explore Selected Works"`.
- **Critical Information Hierarchy**:
  1. Hero: Bold artistic statement, typography-driven or full-screen video reel, availability indicator ("Available for Q3/Q4").
  2. Case Studies (The Core): High-resolution project imagery, client name, services provided, quantifiable business impact.
  3. Capabilities & Approach: Methodology, design philosophy, technical stack.
  4. Industry Recognition: Awards (Awwwards, FWA, Red Dot), publications, client roster.
- **Visual Tone**: Expressive, avant-garde, bold typography, generous whitespace, bespoke interactions.
- **Mobile Priority**: Smooth visual swipe through case studies, fast contact trigger.

## 3. Critical Industry Anti-Patterns

1. **The Ghost Restaurant**: A restaurant website with no food photos, no menu with prices, and no clear street address.
2. **The High-Friction Plumber**: Forcing an emergency plumbing client to fill out a 12-field form before showing a phone number.
3. **The Unverifiable SaaS**: A SaaS platform with zero customer logos, no security certifications, and hidden pricing that forces an enterprise call for basic tiers.
4. **The Frivolous Law Firm**: Using playful neon pastel colors, bouncy animations, and bubbly rounded fonts for a criminal defense or estate law practice.
5. **The Faceless Clinic**: A medical website featuring generic stock photos of smiling models instead of the actual licensed physicians and clinic rooms.

## 4. Pre-Ship Industry Intelligence Checklist

- [ ] Does the information hierarchy match the true primary intent of this vertical's customers?
- [ ] Is the primary CTA tailored specifically to this industry (not a generic "Submit")?
- [ ] Are the required sector-specific trust anchors present (licenses, reviews, food photos, security badges)?
- [ ] Is the visual tone, palette, and typography aligned with the expectations of this market?
- [ ] Are essential logistical details (hours, menu, phone, service area, pricing) immediately discoverable?
